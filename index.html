DUNE · Dynamic Urban Network for Efficiency

What is DUNE?

An AI-powered building management system that understands how spaces are actually used and automatically adjusts energy, water, lighting, cooling and ventilation. Instead of fixed schedules, the building responds to real-time conditions.

How it works

Detect. Understand. Adapt. Monitor.

DUNE connects to the sensors and systems a building already has. No rebuild required.
